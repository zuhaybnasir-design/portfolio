DOCUMENTED DESIGN

From booking to payment

The submitted coursework report describes a four-role Java Swing application with Normal (60-minute) and Major (180-minute) services. Counter staff book appointments with technician-availability checks; technicians mark completed jobs and add feedback; counter staff collect payment; and customers review their service history and comments.

The 56-page report also documents a UML use-case diagram, a class diagram, the abstract User hierarchy, the Reportable interface, enums, and role-themed interfaces. Its screenshots include the actual splash screen, role selector and dashboards.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS

Designed as coursework, not a production service

The report acknowledges that plain-text storage does not support concurrent access, appointment rescheduling is not implemented, record references are not enforced by a database, and the system has no notification service. Password storage demonstrates SHA-256 hashing, not production-grade credential security.
